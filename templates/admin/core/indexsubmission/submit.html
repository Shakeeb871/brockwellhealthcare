Also do these in Google Search Console (5 minutes, high impact):
  1. Open Search Console → your property.
  2. Left menu → Sitemaps → add https://{{ domain }}/sitemap.xml and click Submit.
  3. Top bar → URL Inspection → paste any URL → Request indexing. Do this for your top 10–15 pages (Google limits ~10/day).
  4. Left menu → Pages → check the "Why pages aren't indexed" list a few days later to see what Google actually did.
Building initial links (Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, directories, partner sites) accelerates indexing more than anything else — Google needs an external signal that the site exists and is trusted.
